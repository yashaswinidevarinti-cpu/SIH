❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
  • Detailed explanation of the proposed solution:
    Mausam is a persona-driven hyper-local weather intelligence platform that translates raw atmospheric data into 5 contextual lifestyle lenses:
    • Health & Allergy: Real-time AQI particulate breakdown (PM2.5/PM10), diurnal pollen radar (tree/grass/weed), UV index alerts, and respiratory humidity indices.
    • Outdoor Fitness: Computed 95/100 readiness scores, optimal morning/golden-hour running windows (6:30–8:45 AM), and aerodynamic headwind vectors.
    • Beach & Surfers: Tidal curve harmonics (high/low water levels), wave swell, sea temperatures, and lifeguard safety flags.
    • Travelers & Flights: Inbound flight corridor weather delays and dynamic wardrobe packing checklists.
    • Family & Commute: School drop-off vs pickup micro-forecasts and 60-min precipitation radar.
  • How it addresses the problem:
    Replaces non-actionable raw meteorological numbers with direct daily recommendations, eliminating fragmented checks across multiple weather, AQI, surf, and flight apps.
  • Innovation and uniqueness of the solution:
    Instant persona lens toggling, hyper-local micro-commute forecasting, and a distraction-free Japanese-Scandinavian minimalist aesthetic optimized for mobile.
